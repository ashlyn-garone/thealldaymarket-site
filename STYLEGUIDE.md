# Style guide

## Copy
- **CTA buttons and links are Sentence case** (capitalize only the first word and proper nouns), unless otherwise stated — e.g. "Get a market in your space", "Contact support", "Reach out". Not "Get A Market In Your Space" or "GET A MARKET IN YOUR SPACE".

## Design tokens
Colors, spacing, and type scale are defined as CSS custom properties in `css/styles.css` (`:root`) — treat that file as the source of truth rather than duplicating values here.
