Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

The All Day Market ("All Day Market," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have, when you visit thealldaymarket.com (the "Site") or use our smart market services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you give us. When you fill out a form on our Site — for example, to request a market for your property or to contact support — we may collect your name, email address, phone number, company or property name, physical address, and any other details you choose to share.
  • Information collected automatically. When you visit our Site, we may automatically collect certain information about your device and usage, such as your IP address, browser type, pages viewed, and the date and time of your visit.
  • Cookies and similar technologies. We may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to operate and improve our Site. You can usually set your browser to refuse cookies, though some parts of the Site may not function properly without them.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries and requests, including market installation requests and support tickets;
  • Operate, maintain, and improve our Site and services;
  • Communicate with you about your account, a market at your property, or your inquiry;
  • Send you updates, offers, or other information, where permitted, and which you may opt out of at any time;
  • Monitor and analyze trends, usage, and activity in connection with our Site;
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent transactions and other illegal activity, and protect the rights and property of All Day Market and others.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Service providers who perform services on our behalf, such as hosting, payment processing, email delivery, and analytics. For example, we use Web3Forms to process submissions from forms on this Site; submitted information is transmitted to Web3Forms solely to deliver it to us;
  • Property partners directly related to fulfilling a market request at a specific property, where necessary to provide the service;
  • Legal and safety purposes, where required to comply with the law, a court order, or other legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of All Day Market, our customers, or others; and
  • Business transfers, in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or a portion of our assets.

4. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect the information we collect. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, such as the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information. To make a request, contact us using the information below. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Site and services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, contact us at tom@thealldaymarket.com.
